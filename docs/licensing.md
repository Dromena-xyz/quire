# Licensing

Quire is a paid plugin. One purchase gives you a license key you activate on your
devices. Licensing is designed to stay quiet and keep working offline, so it does
not get between you and your writing.

## Activate

Open **Settings, Quire**, go to **License**, paste your key, and click
**Activate**. Quire confirms the key with the license server once and switches to
its active state. You can press Enter in the key field to activate.

## Devices and activations

A license covers a set number of activations. Each vault on each device uses one,
so the same vault synced to your laptop and desktop counts as two. The settings
panel shows how many activations you have used.

If you reach the limit, free one up with **Deactivate this vault on this device**
on a machine you no longer use it on, then activate the new one.

## Offline by design

After it is activated, Quire keeps working offline. A permanent license never
needs to phone home again, so it stays completely silent. A subscription
re-checks on a relaxed schedule in the background. You do not have to be online to
write.

Use **Check status now** if you ever want to re-confirm immediately, for example
right after a renewal.

## Subscription to permanent

If you bought the monthly subscription, it converts to a permanent license after
ten payments. From then on it behaves like a one-time purchase: no further
charges, and no further check-ins.

## If a license is revoked

A refunded or revoked license locks the plugin the next time it reaches the
server. Your files are never touched: a locked Quire simply stops its paid
features until a valid key is activated. Everything you wrote stays exactly where
it is, as plain Markdown.
