# Licensing

Quire is a paid plugin. One purchase gives you a license key you activate on your
devices. Licensing is built to stay quiet and keep working offline, so it does not
get between you and your writing.

## Activate

Open `Settings > Quire`, go to **License**, paste your key, and click **Activate**
(you can press Enter in the key field). Quire confirms the key with the license
server once and switches to its active state.

## Devices and Activations

A license covers a set number of activations. Each vault on each device uses one,
so the same vault synced to your laptop and desktop counts as two. The License
section shows how many you have used. If you reach the limit, free one with
**Deactivate this vault on this device** on a machine you no longer use it on,
then activate the new one.

## Offline by Design

Once it is activated, Quire keeps working offline, even on a subscription. You do
not have to be online to write.

- A **permanent** license does not re-check on its own. After it is activated it
  stays silent, and only contacts the server again when you activate another
  device or click **Check status now**.
- A **subscription** re-checks on a relaxed schedule in the background, so a
  lapsed or canceled plan is noticed eventually, but a brief time offline never
  interrupts your work.

Use **Check status now** any time you want to re-confirm immediately, for example
right after a renewal.

## Subscription to Permanent

If you bought the monthly subscription, it converts to a permanent license after
ten payments. From then on it behaves like a one-time purchase: no further
charges, and no further check-ins.

## If a License Is Revoked

A refunded or revoked license locks the plugin the next time it reaches the
server. Your writing is never touched: a locked Quire simply pauses its paid
features until a valid key is activated. Everything you wrote stays exactly where
it is, in Obsidian, as plain Markdown notes.
